Under the hood

From spec sheet to price

01

Raw specs

Nine fields — brand, weight, RAM, cameras, processor, battery, screen size, and launch year.

02

Preprocessing

A scikit-learn ColumnTransformer scales numeric fields and encodes brand and processor.

03

XGBoost inference

The transformed vector is passed to a tuned XGBoost Booster, loaded natively.

04

Price estimate

The model outputs a single regression value: the estimated launch price, in ₹.
